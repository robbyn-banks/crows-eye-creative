About:

Robbyn Bentley Walters (b. December 28, 2001) is an American creator, performer, musician, and writer in Grand Rapids, MI. Known for his shenanigans and tomfoolery, he is regarded as a little goofy.

Robbyn received an AFA in Digital Media and a BFA in Digital Art and Design from Kendall College of Art and Design. He plays in the GRCC Campus Band, and is a member of the Transjester Cabaret Troupe where he performs aerials and acrobatics in multiple shows throughout the year.

In the summers, Robbyn is a part of Parks and Recreation and has worked at two state parks. He is passionate about making nature accessible to his community and ensuring everyone can experience the outdoors. In his free time, the Robster enjoys making music, crocheting, and getting outside! Recorded here on this website are all of his creative ventures and muses available for viewing pleasure!
